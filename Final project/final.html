2. Project Concept

RecipePlan is a recipe finder and weekly meal planner for home cooks. A user creates an account, tells the site their diet and allergies once, searches thousands of recipes, saves favourites, plans a week of meals and gets one combined shopping list. Everything is tied to the account, so it works on a phone, a tablet or a laptop.

The recipes come from TheMealDB. The site adds what that service does not have: accounts, preferences, warnings, saved recipes, a planner and a shopping list.

The problem it solves

  • Deciding what to eat takes effort. Recipes are spread across many sites, so people search, compare and forget what they found. RecipePlan gives one place to search, save and revisit them.
  • Diets and allergies make browsing slower and riskier. The site stores preferences once and flags or hides conflicting recipes. Warnings come from keywords in ingredient names, so they are a helper and not a safety guarantee, and the site says so.
  • Planning and shopping are separate chores. RecipePlan builds one merged shopping list from the weekly plan.
  • Notes and bookmarks stay on one device. Accounts keep favourites and plans available everywhere.

Target users

People who cook at home: students, busy workers, and households with dietary needs or allergies. They want to decide quickly what to cook, avoid ingredients they cannot eat, and shop once for the week.

4. Technology Stack

  • HTML, CSS and JavaScript: plain HTML and ES-module JavaScript, with no framework or build step.
  • Styling: Tailwind CSS v3 from the CDN, with Inter and Outfit from Google Fonts.
  • External API: TheMealDB, base URL https://www.themealdb.com/api/json/v1/1.
  • Services: Firebase Authentication for accounts, Cloud Firestore for each user's data, and a Google Translate engine for the language switcher.
  • Hosting: GitHub Pages, deployed from the public/ folder by a GitHub Actions workflow.
  • Repository: kahlen2008/webprogproject, with a main branch for the stable version and a dev branch for daily work.
