AI Is Changing the Way Students Learn

September 12, 2026

Background

Artificial intelligence, or AI, has become an important technology in education. Students can use AI tools to research topics, practice writing, and receive personalized feedback.

Many educators believe that AI can support students when it is used responsibly. Instead of replacing teachers, these tools can provide additional resources for learning.

Analysis

One advantage of AI is that it can provide immediate feedback. For example, a student learning HTML could ask an AI tool to explain what the following code does:

<p>Hello, world!</p>

However, students still need to understand the material themselves. Relying too heavily on AI could reduce opportunities for independent problem-solving and critical thinking.

Technology works best in education when it helps teachers and students accomplish things they could not do as easily on their own.

— Technology Education Review

Key Facts

  • AI can provide personalized learning support.
  • Students can use AI to practice and receive feedback.
  • Teachers remain important for guidance and evaluation.
  • Responsible use is important when working with AI.
