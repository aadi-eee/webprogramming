Mug Rice Krispie Treat Recipe

What You Need

  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 cup mini marshmellows
  • 1 cup Rice krispies cereal

Steps

  1. Put the butter into a microwave-safe mug. Microwave it for 15 to 20 seconds until it melts completely.
  2. Add the marshmallows to the melted butter and stir.
  3. Warning: Microwave for only 20 to 30 seconds until the marshmallows puff up.
  4. Stir the melted marshmallows and butter together until smooth.
  5. Press the mixture gently down into the mug with the back of a spoon. Let it cool for 1 minute to set, then eat!
